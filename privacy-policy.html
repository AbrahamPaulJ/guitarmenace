Guitar Menace: Tab Hand, privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. Guitar Menace has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not have permission to use the internet. It collects, stores on a server, or shares no personal or device data.

Your files

Tab files you open stay on your device, in the app's own storage, so they show up in your song list next time. They are never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Videos you export are saved to your device's Movies folder and go nowhere unless you share them yourself.

The microphone

Practice mode and the tuner listen to your guitar through the microphone, and only while one of them is open. The sound is analysed on your device as it arrives, to tell which note you played, and is then discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The app asks for microphone permission the first time you open either one; you can refuse it, or withdraw it later in Android's settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting anything, this page will say what, why and with whom it is shared before that version is released.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact on the app's Google Play page.
